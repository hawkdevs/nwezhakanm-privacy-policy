# Nwezhakanm Privacy Policy

**Last updated November 26, 2026**

This policy explains how HawkDevs handles information when you use Nwezhakanm on iOS or Android. You can use the app without an account. HawkDevs has no server that receives your app activity, location, or settings. The app does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services, and HawkDevs does not sell personal information.

## Location, compass, and prayer reminders

You can choose a city manually or allow the app to use your device’s location to set your city automatically. The app uses the selected location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. Compass readings help display the direction your device is facing and are processed on the device.

When the app needs to look up a place name or the coordinates of a selected city, it passes the relevant coordinates or city name to your device’s geocoding service. That service may process the request over the network. On iOS, Apple provides the service. On Android, the provider can vary by device. [Apple Location Services and Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/location-services/) · [Android Geocoder](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/location/Geocoder)

The app saves your selected city, coordinates, and calculated Qibla direction on your device. Your prayer reminder preferences are also stored locally, and your device schedules and delivers the notifications.

## Quran pages, recitation, and external links

The app can automatically download missing Quran pages during use so you can read them offline. The pages come from Quran.ws, with GitHub as a fallback. When you play a recitation, audio streams from Quran Foundation’s audio host. The app does not connect to Quran.com or QuranReflect accounts. [Quran.ws](https://quran.ws/) · [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) · [Quran Foundation Privacy Policy](https://quran.com/privacy)

Content hosts may receive your IP address, the page or audio file requested, and standard connection information. The app uses HTTPS for these requests. The app saves your reading progress and playback bookmarks on your device. If you open a tutorial, it opens on YouTube in your browser or the YouTube app. External services handle your visits under their own policies. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Your choices and data deletion

You can switch to manual city selection in the app and revoke location access in your device settings. You can turn off prayer reminders in the app or turn off notification permission in your device settings.

The app keeps locally saved information to remember your settings and progress between sessions. To remove that information and downloaded pages, clear the app’s storage on Android or delete the app from your iPhone. Your device’s backup service may retain copies depending on your backup settings. You can manage those backups through your device or platform account.
